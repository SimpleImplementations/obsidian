### Traits

***Eldritch Mind.*** The warlock has advantage on Constitution saving throws to maintain concentration on a spell.

***Spellcasting.*** The warlock is a 9th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). It regains its expended spell slots when it finishes a short or long rest. All spells cast in this way are cast at the 5th level. It knows the following warlock spells:

- **At Will:** [*booming blade*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/booming-blade), [*eldritch blast*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/eldritch-blast), [*mage armor*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/mage-armor), [*prestidigitation*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/prestidigitation)
- **2/Rest:** [*darkness*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/darkness), [*dispel magic*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/dispel-magic), [*hex*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/hex), [*hold person*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/hold-person), [*mirror image*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/mirror-image), [*sickening radiance*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/sickening-radiance), [*synaptic static*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/synaptic-static)

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The warlock attacks twice with its Eldritch Blast.

***Dagger.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.

***Eldritch Blast.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) force damage. The warlock can also choose to push the creature up to 10 feet away from them in a straight line.
