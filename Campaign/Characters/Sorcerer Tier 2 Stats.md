### Traits

***Metamagic.*** The sorcerer can manipulate their spells in the following ways:

- ***Subtle Spell (3/Day).*** The sorcerer casts a spell, ignoring any somatic or verbal components.
- ***Twinned Spell (2/Day).*** The sorcerer casts a spell that only targets one creature and doesn't have a range of self. The spell can instead target two creatures within the spell's range.

***Spellcasting.*** The sorcerer's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). The sorcerer is a 9th-level spellcaster and can cast the following sorcerer spells:

- **Cantrips (At Will):** [*fire bolt*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/fire-bolt), [*mage hand*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/mage-hand), [*mold earth*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/mold-earth), [*prestidigitation*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/prestidigitation), [*shocking grasp*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/shocking-grasp)
- **1/Day Each:** [*absorb elements*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/absorb-elements), [*greater invisibility*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/greater-invisibility), [*mage armor*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/mage-armor), [*Maximilian's earthen grasp*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/maximilians-earthen-grasp), [*suggestion*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/suggestion), [*synaptic static*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/synaptic-static), [*polymorph*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/polymorph)
- **3/Day Each:** [*chaos bolt*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/chaos-bolt) (2nd level), [*fireball*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/fireball), [*shield*](https://www.aidedd.org/spell/shield)

### Actions

***Dagger.*** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.
