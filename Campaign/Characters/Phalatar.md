### Traits

***Archery Specialist.*** Phalatar has a +2 bonus to attack rolls made with ranged weapons.

***Light Steps.*** Moving through nonmagical difficult terrain costs no extra movement, and Phalatar can move through hazardous plants without taking damage.

***Natural Survivalist.*** Phalatar can't become lost except by magical means, can move stealthily at a normal pace, and is especially good at foraging and tracking. On a successful tracking check, he learns the exact number of creatures, their sizes, and how long ago they passed through the area.

***Spellcasting.*** Wisdom is Phalatar's spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit with spell attacks).

- **3/day:** *ensnaring strike*
- **1/day each:** *hunter's mark*, *locate animals or plants*, *pass without trace*, *snare*

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Phalatar makes two attacks with his shortsword or longbow.

***Shortsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.

***Longbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.

### Bonus Actions

***Nature's Veil (3/day).*** Phalatar magically becomes invisible, along with any equipment he is wearing or carrying, until the start of his next turn.
