Tema: hechicero dracónico de fuego corrompido; mucho daño, mucha presencia, pocas decisiones.

RASGOS
- Draconic Fire Bloodline:
  All his spell damage is fire damage, even if the spell normally deals another damage type. His magic looks like orange flame, black ash, burning chains and magma cracks.

- Legendary Resistance (2/day):
  If Bulzar fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

- Charcoal Body:
  The first time each round a creature hits him with a melee attack from 5 feet, that creature takes 10 fire damage.

- Punches Down:
  If a creature is below half its HP maximum, Bulzar deals +7 fire damage to it with spells and attacks.

EVOCATION SPELLS
All damage is fire damage when Bulzar casts these, even if the spell name says otherwise.

- Cantrips: *fire bolt*, *ray of frost* (white fire), *shocking grasp* (burning spark)
- Level 1: *burning hands*, *magic missile* (chasing embers)
- Level 2: *scorching ray*, *shatter* (heat burst)
- Level 3: *fireball*, *lightning bolt* (fire bolt line)
- Level 4: *wall of fire*, *ice storm* (burning hail)
- Level 5: *cone of cold* (white fire cone)
- Level 6: *chain lightning* (fire chains)
- Level 7: *delayed blast fireball*
- Level 8: *sunburst* (solar fire burst)

ACTIONS
- *Fire Bolt.*
  Ranged spell attack: +12 to hit, range 120 ft., 1 target.
  Hit: 35 (5d10 + 7) fire damage.

- *Fireball* (Recharge 4-6).
  Point within 150 ft.; 20-foot-radius explosion.
  Dexterity save DC 20.
  Damage: 42 (12d6) fire damage, or half on a successful save.

- *Scorching Ray* (3/day).
  Three ranged spell attacks: +12 to hit, range 90 ft.
  Hit: 14 (4d6) fire damage per ray.
  Simple use: split rays, or send all three at the same target.

- *Cone of Cold* (2/day).
  60-foot cone.
  Constitution save DC 20.
  Damage: 45 (10d8) fire damage, or half on a successful save.
  It is still called *cone of cold*, but Bulzar casts it as white fire.

- *Sunburst* (1/day).
  Point within 150 ft.; 20-foot-radius burst.
  Constitution save DC 20.
  Damage: 45 (10d8) fire damage, or half on a successful save.

BONUS ACTION
- Ash Flight (3/day).
  Bulzar flies or moves up to 40 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

REACTION
- Burning Shield (3/day).
  When an attack hits him, Bulzar gains +5 AC against that attack.
  If the attacker is within 10 feet, it takes 14 (4d6) fire damage.

MODO DE USO PARA EL DM
Turno normal:
1. Si hay 3 o más PJ juntos -> *Fireball*.
2. Si están en cono -> *Cone of Cold*.
3. Si hay un objetivo clave o débil -> *Fire Bolt* o *Scorching Ray*.
4. Si el combate tiene que volverse peligroso ya -> *Sunburst*.
5. Usá Ash Flight para mantenerse lejos y Burning Shield contra el primer golpe fuerte.

MODO JEFE
- Ronda 1: *Sunburst* o *Fireball*.
- Ronda 2: *Cone of Cold* si puede agarrar a varios.
- Ronda 3: *Scorching Ray* o *Fire Bolt* al más herido, porque Bulzar siempre castiga al vulnerable.

AJUSTE RÁPIDO
- Más duro: subile los HP a 180.
- Más blando: bajale los HP a 120.
- Más letal: *Fireball* pasa a 14d6.
- Más justo: quitale Punches Down.
