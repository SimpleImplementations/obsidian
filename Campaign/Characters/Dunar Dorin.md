### Traits

***Martial Role.*** The warrior has one of the following traits of your choice:

***Attacker.*** The warrior gains a +2 bonus to attack rolls.

***Defender.*** The warrior gains the Protection reaction below.

***Second Wind.*** The warrior can use a bonus action on its turn to regain hit points equal to 1d10 + 5. If it does so, it can't use this feature again until it finishes a short or long rest.

***Action Surge.*** On its turn, the warrior can take one additional action. Once it uses this feature, it can't use it again until it finishes a short or long rest.

***Improved Critical.*** The warrior's weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

***Extra Attack.*** The warrior attacks twice, instead of once, whenever it takes the Attack action on its turn.

### Actions

***Greataxe (two-handed).*** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d12 + 3) slashing damage.

***Longbow.*** Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.

If the Attacker role is chosen, the attack bonuses become:
Greataxe +8 to hit
Longbow +6 to hit

### Reactions

***Protection (Defender Only).*** The warrior imposes disadvantage on the attack roll a creature within 5 feet of it makes against a target other than the warrior. The warrior must be able to see the attacker.
