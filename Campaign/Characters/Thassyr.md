### Traits

***Evasion.*** If Thassyr is subjected to an effect that allows him to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, he instead takes no damage on a success and half damage on a failure.

***Disciplined Movement.*** Thassyr can move through allied spaces without spending extra movement, and opportunity attacks against him are made with disadvantage while he is not wearing armor.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Thassyr makes three Unarmed Strike attacks.

***Unarmed Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

***Stunning Palm (2/Day).*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of Thassyr's next turn.

### Bonus Actions

***Flurry of Blows (3/Day).*** Thassyr makes one Unarmed Strike attack.

***Step of the Wind (3/Day).*** Thassyr takes the Dash or Disengage action.

### Reactions

***Deflect Strike (3/Day).*** When Thassyr is hit by a weapon attack, he reduces the damage by 15.

### Modo de uso para el DM

1. Si quiere presionar o rematar: Multiattack + Flurry of Blows.
2. Si hay un objetivo peligroso: Stunning Palm.
3. Si queda mal posicionado: Step of the Wind.
4. Guardá Deflect Strike para golpes grandes.
