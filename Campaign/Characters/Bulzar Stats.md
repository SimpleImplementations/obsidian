RASGOS
- Draconic Fire Bloodline:
  All his spell damage is fire damage, even if the spell normally deals another damage type. His magic looks like orange flame, black ash, burning chains and magma cracks.

- Legendary Resistance (2/day):
  If Bulzar fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
