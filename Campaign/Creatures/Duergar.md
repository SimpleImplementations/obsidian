### Traits

***Duergar Resilience.*** The duergar has advantage on saving throws against the Charmed, Paralyzed, and Poisoned conditions.

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the duergar has disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

***Tunnel Fighter.*** The duergar has advantage on melee attack rolls against a creature if both the duergar and the target are within 5 feet of stone walls, worked tunnels, or a natural choke point.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The duergar makes two War Pick attacks.

***War Pick.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage.

***Heavy Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, range 100/400 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d10 + 1) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage.

***Enlarge (Recharge 5-6).*** For 1 minute, the duergar becomes Large, gains advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws, and its War Pick attacks deal an extra 3 (1d6) damage. If there isn't enough room to become Large, it instead gains 10 temporary Hit Points.

***Invisibility (Recharge 6).*** The duergar magically has the Invisible condition until it attacks, casts Enlarge, or its Concentration ends.

### Bonus Actions

***Hook and Drag.*** After the duergar hits a creature with War Pick, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 10 feet straight toward the duergar.
