### Traits

***Sneak Attack (1/Turn).*** The rogue deals an extra 10 (3d6) damage when it hits a target with an attack roll and has Advantage on the roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of the rogue that doesn't have the Incapacitated condition and the rogue doesn't have Disadvantage on the roll.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The rogue makes three attacks, using Shortsword or Hand Crossbow in any combination.

***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage.

***Hand Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +7, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage.

***Poison Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage.

### Bonus Actions

***Cunning Action.*** The rogue takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.

### Reactions

***Uncanny Dodge.*** *Trigger:* The rogue is hit by an attack roll made by a creature it can see. *Response:* The rogue halves the attack's damage against itself (round down).
