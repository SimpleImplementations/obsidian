Converted yetis are frozen corpses dragged back into motion by Orren's necromancy. Their fur hangs in dead clumps over exposed bone, and their eyes glow with a pale blue hunger. They still hunt like yetis, but without breath, fear, or exhaustion.

### Traits

***Undead Fortitude.*** If the yeti is reduced to 0 hit points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is radiant, fire, or from a critical hit. On a success, the yeti drops to 1 hit point instead.

***Fear of Fire.*** If the yeti takes Fire damage, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of its next turn.

***Dead Mountain Climber.*** The yeti has advantage on Strength checks made to climb, grapple, or shove while on ice, stone, or snow.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The yeti can use Bone-Chilling Gaze and makes two attacks, using Claw or Ice Throw in any combination.

***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Cold damage.

***Ice Throw.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +6, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) Cold damage.

***Bone-Chilling Gaze.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature the yeti can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Cold damage, and the target has the Paralyzed condition until the start of the yeti's next turn unless the target has Immunity to Cold damage. *Success:* The target is immune to the Bone-Chilling Gaze of converted yetis for 1 hour.

### Bonus Actions

***Corpse Rush (Recharge 5-6).*** The yeti moves up to half its Speed toward a creature it can see. If it ends this movement within 5 feet of that creature, its next Claw attack this turn has advantage.
