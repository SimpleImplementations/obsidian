### Traits

***Converted Blood.*** Whenever the goliath rolls damage, it deals an extra 3 (1d6) poison damage as part of that damage roll.

***Poisoned Wound.*** A creature that takes poison damage from the goliath has its speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of the goliath's next turn.

### Actions

***Bone Maul.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage.

### Reactions

***Locking Strike.*** When a creature the goliath can see moves out of the goliath's reach, the goliath makes one Bone Maul attack against it. On a hit, the target's speed becomes 0 until the end of the current turn.

***Intercept Blow.*** When a creature the goliath can see within 5 feet is hit by an attack, the goliath can impose disadvantage on that attack roll.
